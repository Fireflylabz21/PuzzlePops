Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2, 2026

Your Privacy Matters to Us

Welcome to Puzzle Pops ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy while providing a fun and entertaining gaming experience. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect information when you play our game.

No Account, Registration, or Login Required to Play

1 Information We Do NOT Collect

Puzzle Pops is designed so you can jump right into gameplay without providing personal identifiers.

  • We do not require user accounts, sign-ins, or social logins.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.
  • We do not collect or store sensitive personal information on our own servers.

2 Information Collected Automatically

While we do not collect personal identifiers directly, third-party services integrated into Puzzle Pops may collect non-personal device information automatically to enable game features, deliver advertisements, and improve performance.

Google Firebase Firestore

We use Firebase Firestore to store game state data, anonymous high scores, game configurations, and non-personal gameplay progress. This data is associated with an anonymous, system-generated installation identifier, not your personal identity.

Google Analytics for Firebase

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how players interact with Puzzle Pops. This includes anonymized metrics such as session duration, level completions, screen views, device model, operating system version, and general geographic location (country/city level).

Advertising Networks (Ad Units)

Puzzle Pops incorporates third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob) to serve banner, interstitial, or rewarded video ads. These services may collect:

  • Unique Mobile Advertising Identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / IDFA).
  • IP address (used primarily to derive approximate location).
  • Ad engagement metrics (views, clicks, impressions).

3 How We Use Collected Information

The non-personal data collected is strictly used for the following operational purposes:

  • To ensure proper functioning and stability of the game.
  • To analyze gameplay patterns, fix bugs, and optimize user experience.
  • To serve non-intrusive or contextual advertisements that support game development.
  • To store anonymous game progress and global leaderboards via Firebase.

4 Third-Party Privacy Links

We encourage you to review the privacy practices of the third-party service providers integrated into our game:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Privacy Information

5 Children's Privacy

Puzzle Pops does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can promptly take corrective action.

6 Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published directly on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We advise you to review this page periodically for updates.
